ApiCon — Master Data Validation

In use · handed over Aug 2026

Automated product master data validation across 25 European markets.

problem

Prices and product names for 25 markets were checked by hand against an Excel reference — about 27 hours per cycle, resting on a few people.

solution

I gathered the requirements with business and data teams and built a Python/Flask tool that compares every field with the live system and ranks deviations by business impact.

result

~1⁠–⁠1.5 hours per cycle. After a security check it went to 15 users; the data team in India agreed to extend it to service contracts. The first run found errors that manual checks had missed.

Apartment Rental Platform

Completed · launch pending

One workflow for rental enquiries, reservations, contracts and move-out.

problem

A private landlord with eight apartments and a seven-room shared flat needed one place to manage availability, enquiries, documents and deadlines.

solution

I built the public website and the management dashboard with AI coding agents (Python, Django, PostgreSQL). Development is complete; launch is pending.

from enquiry to move-out

  1. The enquiry checks availability and blocks double bookings.
  2. Preliminary approval holds the apartment for five days and requests documents.
  3. The dashboard guides review, acceptance and every follow-up step.
  4. Scheduled jobs send emails, track deadlines and delete data under the retention rules.
